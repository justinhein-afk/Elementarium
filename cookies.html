Cookies and storage

Last updated 1 November 2026

Cookies

The game itself sets no cookies. Cloudflare, which hosts the site, may set a cookie that is strictly needed for security, such as telling people from bots. See Cloudflare’s cookie policy.

Local storage

To remember your game, the site uses your browser’s local storage. It stays on your device and isn’t sent to us. It holds:

  • the elements, stars and achievements you have earned;
  • your Journey and Lab progress;
  • your saved worlds, with their names, descriptions and pictures;
  • your settings, such as sound, weather and map size.

Clearing it

To remove everything, clear this site’s data in your browser’s settings. This deletes your progress and saved worlds for good. Private or incognito windows usually forget it all when closed.

Advertising

There are no ads or advertising cookies today. If that changes, this page and the privacy policy will be updated first, and you’ll be asked for consent where the law requires it.
