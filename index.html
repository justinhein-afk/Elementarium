    • Drag paint the selected element · Right-drag erase
    • Scroll brush size · Ctrl + Scroll zoom · Middle-drag or WASD move the map

    • Ctrl + Z undo · Ctrl + Shift + Z redo · Space pause · F full screen
    • On a touch screen: two fingers to zoom and move the map; − and + size the brush
